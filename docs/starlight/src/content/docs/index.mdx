## Key features

- **[Featureless](/featureless/):** Learn features from typed values and nested records inside the model, without a separate feature engineering pipeline.
- **[Modular](/modular/):** Compose models from fields and branches. Extend them with your own custom data types and connect to your own [custom data sources](/advanced/custom-data-modules/).
- **[Scalable](/advanced/distributed-data/):** Stream massive datasets through batched training and inference.
- **[Deployable](/advanced/inference-and-deployment/):** Use the trained model for batch predictions and real-time serving.
- **[SOTA](/benchmarks/):** On the nine published RelArena tasks with over one million training observations, relflow beats TabPFN-3 on nine and TabPFN-3.5 on six.

## Philosophy

relflow starts with a simple idea: the structure and meaning of your data should shape your model.

A typed schema tells relflow how to represent individual values and combine related records. The schema supplies the structure; training learns the patterns that matter for your prediction targets.
