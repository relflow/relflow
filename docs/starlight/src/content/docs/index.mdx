  <p class="home-lead">relflow is a predictive modeling framework, intended for any predictive task. relflow is different from other modeling approaches for relational learning:</p>
</div>

- **[Featureless](/featureless/):** Learn features from typed values and nested records inside the model, without a separate feature engineering pipeline.
- **[Modular](/modular/):** Compose models from fields and branches. Extend them with your own custom leaf types and connect to your own [custom data sources](/advanced/custom-data-modules/).
- **[Scalable](/scaling/):** Stream massive datasets through batched training and inference.
- **[Deployable](/advanced/inference-and-deployment/):** Use the trained model for batch predictions and real-time serving.

As a result of the above unique aspects of relflow, our models are the SOTA: On the nine published RelArena tasks with over one million training observations, relflow beats TabPFN-3 on nine and TabPFN-3.5 on six.

## Philosophy

**relflow was designed to be the first-of-its-class featureless modeling paradigm, providing a scalable solution in which model architecture embodies the latent data structure.**

Our work is motivated by the challenges associated with traditional ML pipelines, which are shared with frontier labs’ recent efforts with in-context learning. These approaches rely on feature engineering loops or even deep-feature synthesis. Such methods prevent economic scaling and deployment, and they require intense engineering and validation efforts.

relflow was built as open-source software and will always be free.
