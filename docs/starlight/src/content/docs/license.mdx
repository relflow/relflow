## What is covered?

Unless a file states otherwise, the license covers project-authored source code,
documentation, examples, schema machinery, cache implementations, and preset
definitions. Third-party components retain their own licenses. The bundled
Libertinus font retains its SIL Open Font License.

## Using and modifying relflow

AGPL allows commercial use, modification, and redistribution under its terms.
Redistributing a covered modified version carries source-sharing obligations.
Section 13 also requires a modified version that supports remote network
interaction to prominently offer its corresponding source to those users.
Corresponding source includes the material needed to build, install, run, and
modify the covered work; a link to an unrelated or unmodified revision does not
describe a custom deployment.

Local private modifications do not carry a general publication requirement.
AGPL does not require submitting improvements upstream. A combined application
that imports or links to relflow may itself be a covered work; the boundary
depends on the integration. Review that boundary before distributing or serving
a proprietary application.

## Data and trained artifacts

Using relflow does not automatically apply AGPL to all inputs or outputs.
Section 2 covers output only when its content constitutes a covered work.
Datasets, schemas supplied by users, predictions, weights, and checkpoints need
to be considered according to their contents and any other applicable licenses.
Their treatment is distinct from the licensing of a training or serving
application that incorporates relflow code.

contributions. This page grants no additional permissions. Ownership of relflow
does not waive the licenses of third-party dependencies.

Before merging outside contributions, the maintainers require a separate written
grant covering the necessary contribution rights. Submitting a pull request or
publishing a fork under AGPL does not by itself grant proprietary relicensing
rights.
