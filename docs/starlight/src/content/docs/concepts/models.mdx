relflow models are novel neural architectures. These models have tree-like
structures with nested collections of both **branches** and **leaves**.

- **[Branches](/concepts/branches/)** are a tool to logically organize collections
  of related data.
- **[Leaves](/concepts/data-types/)** represent arbitrary, individual data points.
  - The premise of relflow data leaves is that any data can be represented,
    exactly as it exists.

## Model Presets

We offer model-presets as opinionated model constructors with specifications for
the dimensionality of a model, the number of attention heads, the number of
layers, and much more. In this way, we hope to abstract away many of the
complexities associated with building initial models.

These pre-sets, though opinionated in nature, are the result of objective HPO &
NAS. These are the products of the maintainer’s efforts in evaluation of the
relflow model factory against industry standard benchmarks for relational
learning.
