relflow models are neural architectures organized as trees of nested branches and
typed leaves. The tree follows the structure of your data: a conversation with
messages, an asset with sensor readings, or a policy with claims and invoices.

- The **root** represents one complete observation.
- **[Branches](/concepts/branches/)** organize collections of related records.
  Each branch can contain its own leaves and nested branches.
- **[Leaves](/concepts/data-types/)** describe individual fields and their leaf
  types, such as numbers, categories, text, sets, or vectors.

You declare this tree with Python classes: `rf.Model` for the root, `rf.Branch`
for related records, and leaf types for the values. The declarations define
the model's structure; training learns how to represent the values and combine
their context.
