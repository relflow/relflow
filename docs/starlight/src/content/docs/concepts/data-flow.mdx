Follow one observation from its source to the returned predictions. Choose a
stage on the connected path, or use **Continue** to follow it step by step.
Switch to **Train** to follow historical answers through loss, gradients, and
parameter updates.

<DataFlow />

Preprocessing and postprocessing are optional; these examples use prepared
records and pass through both stages. The records and predictions are shared
illustrations. Intermediate views show data contracts and model structure.

## Explore the contracts

- [Data modules](/advanced/data-modules/) supply source records and model batches.
- [Leaf Types](/concepts/data-types/) define binding, representations, objectives, and outputs.
- [Model trees](/concepts/models/) and [context branches](/concepts/branches/) organize related information.
- [Runtime](/concepts/runtime/) covers fitting, evaluation, and reading the returned predictions.
