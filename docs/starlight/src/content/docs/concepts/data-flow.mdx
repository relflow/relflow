Follow an observation through the relflow pipeline. Choose a use case, then
open a stage or use **Next** to see what happens to its records and values.

**Predict** follows a fitted model through to its returned results. **Train**
adds historical answers and follows the loss, gradients, and parameter updates.
Preprocessing and postprocessing are optional; these examples use prepared
records and pass through both stages.

The records and predictions are shared illustrations. Intermediate views show
data contracts and model structure.
